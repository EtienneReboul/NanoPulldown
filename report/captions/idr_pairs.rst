Bait IDR x prey IDR physicochemical complementarity (``nanopulldown.annotate.idr_pairs``,
SPARROW / ALBATROSS): opposite charge blocks, cation-pi and pi-pi stickers,
hydrophobic patches. Each score is ranked against the same IDR paired with random
background IDRs (percentile; 1 = more complementary than every random partner) and,
for local scores, against composition-preserving shuffles (z).
The **mechanism heatmap** shows the top IDR pairs (rows) x the four mechanisms.
The **#NN IDR profiles** figures show, per top pair, windowed NCPR, aromatic and
R + K fractions, and AIUPred disorder + MoRFs on the bait (left) and prey (right) chains.
The tables list every IDR pair and the control pairs (histone H1 x prothymosin alpha).
A heuristic ranking of candidates, not a binding energy.

Whether the predicted complexes actually bring the paired IDRs into contact is
drawn per pair in **PLIP contacts on SPARROW IDR pairs** (one subsection per
bait).
