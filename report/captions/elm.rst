ELM short linear motifs (``nanopulldown.annotate.elm``): motifs in the disordered
regions of one side of a pair whose ELM interaction domain (Pfam) is on the other
side. Sequence-level candidates only. Whether a predicted complex puts the motif
on that domain is drawn per pair in **PLIP contacts on sequences of interest**
(subsection *ELM motifs*: ticks per motif residue, filled when contacted, thick when
the contact is on the partner's binding domain).
